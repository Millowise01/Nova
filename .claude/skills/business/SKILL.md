# Business

## When to use this skill

- Market research, competitive analysis, financial modeling, business strategy, or product/business analysis.

## Checklist / best practices

- Ground market/competitive claims in credible, cited sources.
- State every assumption behind a projection or model explicitly.
- Present a range or sensitivity analysis instead of false precision for uncertain figures.
- Cross-check a sizing/financial estimate with a second method where feasible.
- Distinguish confirmed fact from inference about a competitor's strategy or intent.
- Connect the analysis to a concrete, actionable recommendation.
- Note explicitly when output is informational analysis, not licensed financial/legal advice.

## Common pitfalls

- A TAM/SAM/SOM number with no stated methodology.
- Treating a competitor's marketing claim as verified fact.
- A financial projection with no stated assumptions.
- Strategic advice with no acknowledgment of what could make it wrong.

## Standards & references

- State methodology and assumptions alongside any quantitative business claim.
