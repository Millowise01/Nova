# Project Management

## When to use this skill

- Planning a project, breaking down a body of work, tracking execution, or communicating status.

## Checklist / best practices

- Break work into tasks small enough to estimate and track independently.
- Sequence tasks by real dependency, not assumed or convenient order.
- Surface blockers and at-risk items proactively, not after they've already slipped.
- State assumptions and risks explicitly alongside a plan or timeline.
- Keep the plan/status current as new information arrives.
- Communicate status concisely, matched to the audience (exec summary vs. detailed).
- Make trade-offs explicit when scope, time, and quality can't all fit.

## Common pitfalls

- A status update that hides risk to avoid an uncomfortable conversation.
- A plan with no accounting for dependencies between workstreams.
- Scope creep absorbed silently instead of being called out.
- A single point of failure (one person, one system) left unflagged.

## Standards & references

- Status should default to honest and specific over reassuring and vague.
