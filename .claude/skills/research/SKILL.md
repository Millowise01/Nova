# Research

## When to use this skill

- Any task that requires finding, verifying, or synthesizing external information.

## Checklist / best practices

- Clarify exactly what question the research needs to answer.
- Prefer primary and authoritative sources over secondhand summaries.
- Cross-check non-trivial claims across more than one source.
- Separate established fact from estimate, projection, or opinion explicitly.
- Note disagreement between sources rather than silently picking one.
- Cite sources for specific claims, not just a general reading list.
- Flag what could not be verified instead of presenting it as settled.

## Common pitfalls

- Fabricating a citation, statistic, or quote.
- Treating a single source's claim as consensus.
- Presenting a stale figure as current without checking its date.
- Confusing a vendor/marketing claim with independent verification.

## Standards & references

- Never fabricate a source. If a fact can't be verified, say so.
